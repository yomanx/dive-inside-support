Help with your voyage, purchases, and saved progress.

Contact support

Email oops11.adv@gmail.com. Include your device model, system version, game version, and a description of what happened. Please do not send your Apple Account password, payment card information, or verification codes.

Purchases and the month pass

The updated Apple version offers a one-month pass as a single payment. It does not renew automatically. The purchase screen shows the price in your App Store currency, the included content, and the expiry date after purchase. Progress you earn remains saved when the pass ends. Existing verified purchases and legacy subscriptions continue to be recognized.

Use Restore Purchases in the game while signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase. Apple handles payments and refund requests. Manage any legacy renewing subscription in Apple Account subscriptions. Buying a new non-renewing pass does not cancel an older subscription.

Saved progress

Progress and preferences are stored on your device. When available, Apple iCloud synchronizes supported progress. Keep iCloud enabled on the devices you want to synchronize. Resetting progress in the game is intended to erase that progress; keep a device backup before troubleshooting.
